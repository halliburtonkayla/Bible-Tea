    The heart behind the tea

    I know what it’s like to open the Bible and get lost in the names, the places, and who’s related to who. So I tell the story the way I’d tell you something that happened: who did what, how we got here, and what made me stop and think.

    The tea is my conversational retelling, not a replacement for Scripture. Each section links to its Bible passage. Book introductions help you get your bearings; the story collection goes deeper into the stories already told.
